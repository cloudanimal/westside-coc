Who We Are

Simple, New Testament Christianity.

Westside church of Christ is a congregation of believers in Newberry, Florida. We strive to worship God the way the first Christians did — with heartfelt, a cappella singing, prayer, the Lord’s Supper each first day of the week, giving, and the preaching of God’s Word.

We hold the Bible as our only guide, and we aim to wear the name of Christ without denominational division. Our desire is simply to be Christians — nothing more and nothing less.
